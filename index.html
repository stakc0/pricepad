The print

up pot + down pot == the SOL in the market's vault

Pick

Any of the 68 series, at any leverage from 1x to 50x. Eggs swing hard, so 2x is plenty. Toilet paper barely moves, so 20x makes a month of it worth having an opinion about.

Back a side

Your SOL goes into the up pot or the down pot, and you get that side's token at what the pot is worth right now. No order book, no counterparty to find, no fee.

Get paid

When the next figure lands, the losing pot pays the winning one: the move times the leverage, never more than 99%. Sell whenever you like, outside release hour.

Questions

What am I actually buying?

A token that is a share of one side's pot. EGG5UP is a share of the up pot on the 5x eggs market, and selling it back pays you that share in SOL. You are not buying eggs. You are on one side of a published number, against whoever is on the other.

Where do the numbers come from?

The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Groceries and fuel are its average price series (dollars per pound, gallon or dozen). Everything else is a CPI item index. Every market links to its series on bls.gov, so any figure can be checked in one click.

How much does a print move?

The size of the move times the market's leverage, as a share of the losing pot, capped at 99%. Eggs up 2% on a 5x market moves 10% of the down pot across the line. A flat month moves nothing, and if nobody is on one side, nothing moves at all.

Why can't I sell during release hour?

Because the only people who want to sell in that hour already know the answer. The freeze starts an hour before the scheduled release, ends as soon as the figure is on chain, and can never run past three days after the release even if the figure never arrives.

Who puts the number on chain?

One attester key. The program lets it add a newer month and announce the next release, and nothing else: it cannot rewrite a month, cannot post a month that has not ended, and has no instruction that touches a market's SOL. It could post a wrong figure, so check any print against its source.

What are the fees?

None. Launching a market costs its account rent, and trades cost Solana network fees. The program takes nothing from a pot.

Is this real money?

Not yet. Balances are play SOL until the Solana program is deployed to mainnet.
